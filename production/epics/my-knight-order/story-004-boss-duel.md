## Implementation Notes (2026-11-05)

- Decision (user, 2026-11-05): the duel is a joust in repeated passes (마상 창시합 왕복). The rules and numbers
  below were approved as drafted.
- Pass timeline (`duel_rules.json`): the riders ride at each other for `approach_seconds` (2.4); the boss's
  shield side (high or low) is shown `guard_reveal_seconds` (1.4) before the impact; the judgement window is the
  last `window_seconds` (0.3) before the impact, judged by Story 002's `ChargeTimingJudge`; the next pass starts
  `turnaround_seconds` (1.0) after the impact. The shield follows a fixed per-boss pattern
  (`boss_guard_pattern`, repeated), so a boss can be learned; there is no randomness.
- Input: one tap per pass; upper half of the screen = lance high, lower half = lance low. Only the first tap
  between the shield reveal and the impact counts.
- Resolution per pass: Success = lance x `success_multiplier` (1.0) and the boss's lance is deflected; Early =
  lance x `early_multiplier` (0.4) and the boss's lance hits; no tap = no strike and the boss's lance hits.
  Aiming at the shield multiplies the strike by `guarded_multiplier` (0.25). So with no input the protagonist
  loses (4 passes with the sample data).
- Stats: lance power = round(rider_attack x (1 + horse_speed x `speed_power_scale`)); duel health = rider_hp +
  horse_hp. Sample (`duel_sample_setup.json`): protagonist 30/100 + horse 20/40 = power 42, health 140; boss
  25/120 + horse 20/40 = power 35, health 160; victory reward 3 growth points.
- End: health 0 for the protagonist = defeat (also when both fall on the same pass); boss health 0 = victory with
  `DuelResult.GrowthReward`; `max_passes` (12) with both up = defeat (`ReachedPassCap`). Story 007 connects
  defeat to the end of the run.
- Code: pure C# `KnightOrder.Duel.Core` (`DuelSimulation`, no engine references), `KnightOrder.Duel.Loading`
  (`DuelDataLoader`, snake_case DTOs), `KnightOrder.Duel.Presentation` (`DuelView`, `DuelSceneLauncher`,
  `DuelEvidenceCapture`). `Assets/Scenes/Duel.unity` is generated by `BattleSceneBuilder` and is second in the
  build list; the player opens it with `--scene Duel` until Story 007 links it into the run.
- View: isometric list (two lanes and a barrier), mounted protagonist (blue) and boss (red, slightly larger);
  gold shield on the boss, a timing bar, the lance tilts with the aim; PERFECT / EARLY / SHIELD / NO STRIKE and
  PARRIED / HIT popups with tones; VICTORY (+growth) / DEFEAT banner, a tap restarts.
- Not verified: a real mouse click / real touch device on the built player (scripted taps only, through the same
  `DuelSimulation.Tap`); audio.
- Brief updated (user approved, 2026-11-05): `design/game-brief.md` Core loop now states the joust rule under the
  boss duel, and the mounted-unit rule (only the protagonist and heroes ride, one per tile) under the battle.
